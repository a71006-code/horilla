# California Small Employer Payroll Reporting Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** After the design and scope are approved, use `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` or `superpowers:executing-plans` to implement the approved tasks. Steps use checkboxes for tracking.

**Status:** The six draft reporting workflows are implemented for the 2026 California scope. The final Superpowers code review cleared the changes after fixes, and 122 payroll tests passed on an isolated SQLite upgrade from the tracked baseline. Software acceptance uses synthetic payroll, filed totals, and deposits. The NAS PostgreSQL upgrade is a separate deployment check.

**Goal:** Run payroll in Horilla for one California employer with fewer than ten employees and produce reconciled summaries for Forms 941, 940, DE 9, DE 9C, W-2, and W-3.

**Architecture:** Keep Horilla's existing payment workflow. Capture the employer, actual payment date, and reviewed tax facts from paid payslips. Build each report from those facts and confirmed tax deposits. Complete the reporting implementation before its full code review and Git handoff.

**Tech stack:** Existing Django and PostgreSQL stack, Python `Decimal`, standard CSV tooling, and the existing `TaxFormFiller` where the selected template works.

**Design context:** [Existing US/CA minimum design](../specs/2026-05-27-us-ca-payroll-minimum-design.md) and [W-2/W-3 export plan](2026-06-01-w2-w3-year-end-exports.md). This draft preserves their reuse-first direction and proposes strengthening the paid payroll data before treating their exports as filing support.

## Implementation progress

- The isolated 2026 paycheck calculator now composes standard California hourly overtime, regular-wage federal and California withholding, and the employee and employer contributions. The caller supplies approved worked minutes or gross salary, classified wage bases, earlier paid wages, and employer notice rates. It keeps FIT and Additional Medicare separate and returns the contribution wage bases with the amounts. FUTA remains before credits.
- Existing tax and cash-requirements reports can read both numeric and legacy nested taxable gross values. The W-2 builder now preserves the California SDI label when rounding amounts.
- `RecordedCaliforniaPaycheck` and `record_paid_ca_paycheck` now provide the paid-record backend. The service checks paid status and verifies gross and net against the supplied calculation. It stores the explicit employer and check date, per-tax wages and amounts, prior paid YTD, and employer notice rates as a write-once snapshot through the application model. The report selector uses the recorded employer and actual payment date. PostgreSQL is the target for concurrent posting.
- A superuser can open **Review California tax facts** from a paid payslip, enter the actual payment date, legal employer, classified wages and notice rates from the verified register, and preview the result. A signed preview binds the inputs, reviewer, employee, and payment details. Recording verifies those details again under lock and stores the reviewer's account.
- **California quarterly worksheet** in Payroll Report shows draft 2026 wages and liabilities from recorded checks, federal liability by payment day and month, and California PIT wages and withholding by employee. Confirmed 941 and DE 9 deposits are recorded with the actual payment date and the quarter to which the agency applied the payment. Identical confirmations are idempotent. Voiding a confirmation preserves the reviewer and reason.
- The quarterly return preview calculates Form 941 and DE 9 balances from the recorded wages and deposits plus verified rates, current-quarter adjustments, and applied credits. The draft CSV includes those lines. With a current quarter review, the preparer can apply verified dated changes to monthly or daily liability and export the resulting schedule once it ties to Form 941 line 12.
- **California year-end worksheet** in Payroll Report now groups recorded 2026 wage and withholding amounts by employee, sums supported W-2 boxes to W-3, and shows annual and quarterly gross FUTA before credits. Confirmed 940 deposits are recorded by year and cannot duplicate 941 payments with the same confirmation reference. Annual cap breaches block the draft. Quarterly and annual worksheet CSVs omit protected filing identifiers.
- New paid-check reviews capture verified EDD subject wages and standard plan code S separately from California PIT wages. The quarterly DE 9C rows show missing source fields on older checks explicitly. A superuser can supply the missing fields from the verified register on the paid-check review screen. Horilla stores the reviewer and review time with the supplemental record and retains the original check facts.
- The **California employer filing profile** now records verified EIN and EDD account numbers separately, plus the legal employer name and federal return type. Quarterly review compares independently entered payroll register and agency deposit totals with recorded sources and captures the verified 941 schedule and headcount and the three DE 9C headcounts. Changes to the contributing checks, deposits, or profile require a fresh review. The current review details appear on the draft CSV.
- The year-end worksheet compares each paid quarter's supported W-3 wage and income tax withholding amounts to independently transcribed filed 941 and DE 9C totals. Missing quarters and differences appear in the worksheet and CSV. Replacement totals require confirmation and retain the earlier entry. A changed filing profile requires fresh entries. The comparison uses aggregate DE 9C amounts and covers regular cash wages.
- Paid-check recording and both worksheets validate that recorded year-to-date wage bases do not overlap. Enter same-day checks in payment order. Opening balances and gaps between checks still require verification against the external payroll register. The year-end worksheet compares Form 941 FICA after the signed fractions-of-cents adjustment with employee withholding plus the employer share calculated on quarterly wages. It flags sick pay, tips, and group-term life insurance adjustments for preparer review.
- Legacy tax exports require an active superuser and disable response caching. Generic employee editing, bulk updates, spreadsheet exports, and API responses exclude SSNs. Employee admin limits the SSN field to superusers.
- Container startup stops if migrations fail. For an empty deployment, run `python manage.py createhorillauser` interactively in the container to create the first administrator.
- The Form 940 preview maps recorded cash wages to federal wage and tax lines and accepts the final California Schedule A rate, any preparer-computed line 10 adjustment, and applied prior-year credit. It includes the summary in the draft CSV. A missing final rate leaves the affected total and balance blank.
- The W-2/W-3 handoff adds preparer-verified special entries, including reported cash tips, qualified federal overtime premiums, and occupation codes. Missing entries stay unresolved. CASDI comes from the recorded paid checks. The employer filing profile links the worksheet employee IDs to masked identities and protected admin records.
- The employee DE 9C reconciliation compares transcribed filed amounts with recorded employees for the selected quarter, shows missing, unexpected, and mismatched rows, and preserves revisions. The CSV uses internal employee IDs.
- **Report history** retains exact reviewed CSVs with reviewer, timestamp, worksheet revision, and source record IDs. The reviewer can attach agency confirmation references to the archived return. Subsequent changes preserve the prior reviewed copy.
- Report history retains the employer identity revision and the normalized review inputs used to generate the CSV. Incomplete drafts remain available for download. Confirmation eligibility depends on the applicable quarter or year checks. Voiding a mistaken confirmation preserves the receipt, reason, and reviewer.
- A synthetic nine-employee close verifies both quarterly and year-end CSVs, including the FUTA and UI wage caps, all DE 9C employee rows, and the W-2-to-W-3 rollup.
- Build from the clean Git handoff so unrelated local migrations stay outside the image. Supply deployment settings through the environment. Verify the upgrade on a disposable PostgreSQL copy of the NAS database before live redeployment.
- Run the calculation tests without a database using `python -m unittest payroll.tests.test_ca_hourly payroll.tests.test_ca_withholding payroll.tests.test_us_ca_contributions payroll.tests.test_federal_withholding payroll.tests.test_ca_payroll_check`.

## Accountant handoff in Payroll Report

1. Verify the legal employer in **California employer filing profile**. Match employee record IDs to the protected employee records and verify the applicable name, SSN, and mailing address.
2. Review paid checks against the payroll register. Select the legal employer and paid quarter in **California quarterly worksheet**, reconcile confirmed deposits, and record the quarter review. Preview the 941 and DE 9 lines with verified adjustments, rates, and credits. For a monthly or semiweekly IRS schedule, reconcile the liability schedule to line 12 before exporting it.
3. Use the quarterly DE 9C rows for wage reporting. Once the report is filed, compare its employee amounts with the recorded amounts in **DE 9C reconciliation**. Include subject wages and wage plan code for the full comparison.
4. In **California year-end payroll worksheet**, compare W-2 and W-3 amounts with filed quarterly returns. Enter verified special W-2 fields, download the preview CSV, and resolve any missing entries with the preparer. Preview the Form 940 lines using the published Schedule A rate and applicable state UI adjustments and prior credits.
5. Use **Save reviewed draft** after reviewing each preview. After filing through the existing agency or preparer workflow, attach the confirmation reference to that version in **Report history**.

Submit Forms 941 and 940 through an authorized IRS filing channel or preparer. The IRS lists its business tax account, Direct Pay for businesses, EFTPS, and other electronic transfer options for federal tax deposits. [IRS employment tax filing and deposits](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/e-file-employment-tax-forms)

Use California e-Services for Business for DE 9 and DE 9C. For W-2, use SSA Business Services Online or the preparer’s filing channel and retain the W-3 totals for reconciliation. [EDD filing and payment](https://edd.ca.gov/eservices) · [SSA W-2/W-3 filing checklist](https://www.ssa.gov/employer/W2checklist.htm)

## Scope and approaches

- **Selected handoff:** Reviewed summaries for manual entry or accountant handoff. Provide line-by-line worksheets and CSVs, plus reviewed PDF previews or employee W-2 copies where the applicable templates are verified. Keep filing, employee delivery, tax payments, and payment confirmations in their existing external workflows.
- **Proposed employer scope:** One legal employer and EIN, one California EDD payroll account, California W-2 employment, normal commercial payroll, and a single chosen launch tax year. Use 2026 as a reference fixture where final instructions are available. Confirm the actual launch year before loading production rules.
- **Harden the current exports only:** This would ship sooner, but preserving live reconstruction from payslip period dates and editable deduction settings would leave historic amounts and quarter boundaries unreliable. Reuse the screen and renderers and add the minimum persisted tax data required to make the outputs repeatable.
- **Add agency upload files:** This can follow reconciliation. It requires separate, year-specific acceptance work for each supported format, such as SSA EFW2 and any EDD format selected for the employer. Direct submission and payment would be a separate project with its own authorization, acceptance, and receipt handling.
- **Employer profile to confirm:** First required quarter and tax year, first payroll on Horilla, employee pay frequencies and actual pay dates, IRS 941 versus 944 assignment, federal deposit schedule, employer's DE 2088 rates and coverage, existing payroll provider and YTD registers, and actual compensation/benefits. Confirm overtime, bonuses, tips, pretax benefits, retirement, and owners or special coverage with the payroll reviewer. Implement the classifications the company uses and block unsupported cases until reviewed. Employee count alone does not determine filing obligations or the federal deposit schedule.

## Milestone 1 · Establish the paid payroll source

- [ ] **Create a reproducible baseline.** In an isolated test database, run `python manage.py test payroll.tests.test_federal_tax_w4 payroll.tests.test_report_employer_liability payroll.tests.test_year_end_tax_exports`. Record failures before changing behavior and replace assertions that preserve known withholding errors with examples from the official instructions. Check the deployed migration graph against local branches before creating schema migrations. Inventory deployed PDF revisions and employer setup through the payroll administrator. Use synthetic employee identities for development and test exports.
- [ ] **Prove the time-to-gross-pay path before report work.** `project/models.py` records project and task hours. Payroll's hourly path reads validated Attendance instead, and `payroll/methods/methods.py` subtracts recorded overtime from regular hours before a separate configured allowance may add pay for it. The overtime in `attendance/models.py` is measured against the scheduled minimum. Use Attendance as the proposed payroll time source if it matches the actual workflow. If the company uses project time or an external register, define one approved import into the payroll time source and prevent double counting. Reconcile punches, manual edits, missed punches, leave and required overtime before approval. Preserve the approved time records and effective pay rate behind each posted earning. Test ordinary hourly pay, daily and weekly overtime without double counting, double time, seventh-day work, paid versus unpaid leave, and a salary period. Classify exemption separately from pay type. Cover salaried nonexempt and relevant bonuses if used. The time approval flag alone cannot determine whether worked time is payable. [California DLSE overtime guidance](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/FAQ_Overtime.htm).
- [ ] **Version employer settings and tax rules.** Extend payroll tax settings (`payroll/models/models.py`, `payroll/models/tax_models.py`, and existing settings forms) with explicit legal employer identifiers, effective dates, official rule revisions, employer UI/ETT setup, and verified deposit schedule. Record the selected rules at posting. Missing rules or identity block report release. Rates and forms can be updated for a new year without changing a closed year. [IRS Publication 15](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15), [IRS Publication 15-T](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t), [EDD rates and withholding](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Rates_and_Withholding), [EDD taxable wages](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Determine_Taxable_Wages/).
- [ ] **Post a durable tax record.** Add an additive reporting record linked to the payslip or equivalent normalized paid facts on it. Persist employer at time of payment, employee ID, work period, actual check date, rule revision, and immutable per-tax facts. Store gross and includable/exempt earnings, each distinct wage base, employee withholding and employer liability separately, and required classification details. Use `Decimal` with documented rounding. Store deposits in separate records with agency, tax type, effective date, period applied, amount, and payment reference. Model carry-forward credits explicitly. Avoid a second payroll engine.
- [ ] **Reconcile paid records for reporting.** Use the existing paid payslip and verified payroll register to review the actual check date, employer, wage bases, and YTD. Preserve a stable order for same-day payslips and make recording retries idempotent. Record report corrections with an audit reason and applicable tax period. Payroll approval and payment remain in the existing workflow.
- [ ] **Validate withholding before payroll goes live.** Review current W-4/FIT logic in `payroll/methods/tax_calc.py` and `federal_tax.py` against the selected year's Pub. 15-T and CA PIT logic in `payslip_calc.py` against EDD DE 4/withholding schedules. Test the missing-certificate defaults, supported pay frequencies, and W-4 inputs against agency examples. Separate FIT from Additional Medicare, apply the employer withholding threshold using paid YTD wages, and preserve employee and employer FICA separately. Track YTD per employee, legal employer, year, and tax base. Apply UI, ETT, FUTA, and Social Security caps to the right bases rather than gross pay across the board. CA SDI has no wage cap for years beginning in 2024. Test pretax treatment by tax type instead of assuming that all pretax deductions reduce all wage bases. Use the same posted tax facts for payroll and reporting. Include a regression case for the current mismatch between the numeric `taxable_gross_pay` written by payroll and the nested object expected by the tax-report builder. [IRS Publication 15](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15), [IRS Publication 15-T](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t), [EDD rates](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Rates_and_Withholding).
- [ ] **Backfill only verifiable history.** For a midyear move, load prior payroll by check date, employer, employee, and tax base from approved payroll registers. Use transaction detail for periods the system must regenerate and use opening YTD for prospective caps only where its provenance and limits are documented. Import or reconcile deposits separately to payment records. Flag duplicate or unmatched entries and missing facts. Never infer actual check dates from work-period end or recalculate prior payments using today's tax rules.

**Acceptance:** Trace one hourly check and one salaried check from approved time or contract earnings through gross pay, tax-specific wage bases, withholding, employer taxes, net pay, and the applicable report lines. Reconcile against independently calculated expected amounts. A December 2026 pay period paid in January 2027 belongs to the 2027 paid-wage reports. Two employers cannot share a report or wage cap. A repeated posting creates one record. Changing deduction settings does not change already posted figures. A draft, unpaid confirmed slip, or unresolved legacy payment cannot silently enter a released report. Reversal and replacement are traceable to the original.

## Milestone 2 · Finish quarterly reports

- [ ] **Build shared report selection.** Require a legal employer and an explicit quarter or tax year. Read posted facts from that period with consistent treatment of adjustments. Leave arbitrary range filters available for internal payroll analytics. Produce typed report payloads with a source record trail, rule revision, completeness checks, and human-readable blocking errors. Add pure builders under `report/forms/` and let the current `report/views/payroll_report.py` routes call them. For each form, maintain a line or box checklist for the supported revision. Identify the source, rounding rule, and whether the field is computed, supplied by the reviewer, or inapplicable. Require explicit review of inapplicable fields so blanks cannot hide gaps.
- [ ] **941.** Build wage and withholding lines for FIT, Social Security, Medicare, and Additional Medicare with applicable adjustments. Count employees using the designated pay period that includes the 12th of the final month of the quarter. Use actual pay-date tax liability for monthly Line 16 or daily Schedule B when required. Reconcile liabilities to the applicable return total and reconcile deposits or credits separately to balance due. Handle supported zero wage periods and route employers assigned to Form 944 or unsupported filing cases for review. [IRS 941 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i941), [IRS Schedule B instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i941sb).
- [ ] **DE 9 and DE 9C.** Build a pair from the same quarter and state account. DE 9 totals show applicable subject wages, UI/ETT taxable wages and contributions, SDI, PIT withholding and deposits or credits. DE 9C shows each reportable employee's identity, applicable wage plan code, subject wages, PIT wages, and PIT withheld. Count reportable employees for the pay period containing the 12th of each month. Preserve zero payroll reporting and verified adjustments. Ensure the DE 9C totals reconcile to the corresponding DE 9 totals, without equating different wage bases. [EDD Employer’s Guide](https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/pdf_pub_ctr/de44.pdf), [EDD required filings](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/Required_Filings_and_Due_Dates/).

**Acceptance:** A fixture with different monthly effective withholding rates fails gross-pay proration and passes actual liability allocation. A nine-employee DE 9C contains all nine wage rows with correct page/grand totals if rendered as PDF. Test quarter-crossing pay, an employee hired or terminated around the 12th, zero payroll, deposits differing from tax due, and a cap crossed during the quarter.

## Milestone 3 · Finish annual reports

- [ ] **940 and Schedule A.** Compute full-year total payments, exempt payments, per-employee excess wages, taxable FUTA, credit adjustments for state coverage or late payment, quarterly liability and carryovers, deposits, and balance. Pin California credit reduction to the final official Schedule A for that tax year when applicable. Mark the calculation provisional if the final rate or state payment history is missing. Do not treat a prior-year rate as the current one. [IRS 940 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i940), [EDD FUTA](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/federal-unemployment-tax-act).
- [ ] **W-2 and W-3.** Refactor `report/forms/year_end_tax.py` to build employee-year rows from paid facts. Preserve separate federal income, Social Security, Medicare, and California PIT wage bases and associated withholdings. Include applicable CA SDI treatment and year-specific fields. For 2026, verify the revised Box 14 and the applicable qualified overtime or tip reporting fields. Build W-3 from the final employee rows, and reconcile federal taxable wages and withholding to the four quarterly 941s. Compare only like amounts and account for employer-side FICA and documented adjustments. Also reconcile W-2 state PIT totals to the corresponding DE 9C reports. [IRS W-2/W-3 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3).

**Acceptance:** Two payments crossing a FUTA cap and three with different federal, FICA, and state taxable wages produce independently verified results. W-3 ties to all W-2s exactly at the published rounding, including two employees who share a name. The four quarter-to-year comparisons explain permitted adjustments and block unresolved differences. A year with missing final California credit reduction cannot appear ready for filing.

## Milestone 4 · Deliver, reconcile, and pilot

- [ ] **Complete the report package.** In the current Payroll Report screen, select employer and period, show calculation completeness, line/box detail and reconciliation exceptions, and provide reviewed worksheets and CSVs for all six forms. Test the handoff with the payroll reviewer using the corresponding agency screens or preparer workflow. For optional PDFs, pin agency, tax year/revision, expected fields, and approved deployment template. Test rendered values for the actual templates, including XFA or AcroForm compatibility and DE 9C pagination. Use unique, nonsensitive filenames and an explicit missing-output error instead of a silently incomplete ZIP. Label the supported use of each export. Missing optional templates must not block reviewed worksheets or CSVs.
- [ ] **Protect tax identity and filing history.** Before loading real SSNs, restrict tax identity fields in `employee/forms.py` and `horilla_api/api_serializers/employee/serializers.py`. Generic employee access must not expose a full SSN. Enforce employer-scoped payroll tax report permissions independently of the selected UI filter. Mask SSNs in ordinary previews, require authorized download for complete identifiers, prevent response caching, and keep sensitive values out of filenames, URLs, and audit messages. Store reviewed report versions with source record IDs, rule/template versions, preparer, reviewer, and external filing confirmation. Retain originals when recording corrections. Route formal 941-X, DE 9/DE 9C adjustments, and W-2c/W-3c through the agency or accountant in this release.
- [ ] **Confirm filing handoff.** EDD generally requires electronic submission of DE 9/DE 9C and deposits, so use the worksheets for e-Services for Business entry or preparer handoff. For W-2, use SSA Business Services Online or a preparer and retain W-3 totals for reconciliation. W-3's transmission depends on the filing channel. The federal electronic filing threshold counts applicable information returns together, including W-2s and 1099s. An ordinary CSV or a generated worksheet PDF is not an agency upload format. Document approved IRS filing and deposit channels for 941 and 940 in the operator checklist. [EDD electronic filing mandate](https://edd.ca.gov/en/payroll_taxes/e-file_and_e-pay_mandate_for_employers/), [SSA filing options](https://www.ssa.gov/faqs/en/questions/KA-01759.html), [IRS Form 940 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i940), [IRS Form 941 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i941).
- **Software close.** Compare synthetic payroll, filed amounts, and deposits in an isolated database. Check quarterly wage and withholding reconciliation, annual cap consumption, adjustments and carryovers, and the W-2 to W-3 rollup.
- **Live-use verification.** The operator verifies the NAS PostgreSQL upgrade and the applicable agency figures, identities, and rates before relying on a worksheet for an actual filing.

**Release gate:** A reviewer can trace each reported amount to posted pay or a recorded adjustment or deposit. Each selected export is present, correct for the employer and period, and matches the reconciled worksheet. Automated checks include cross-employer denial, missing-identity failure, repeatable historical exports, zero-payroll cases, cent rounding, nine employee rows, and protected SSN handling. The payroll reviewer signs off on the period, official revisions, and supported earnings and deductions. A later electronic export project can reuse the same reconciled payloads.
